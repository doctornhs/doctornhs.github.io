Shoulder

Hee Seung Nam, MD, Orthopedic Surgeon · Area of practice

I treat rotator cuff tears, shoulder dislocation, calcific tendinitis and arthroscopic surgery. Because the cause of shoulder pain can lie outside the shoulder, I look at the whole body when the images and the exam do not match.
