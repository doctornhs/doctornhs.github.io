Fractures & Sports Injuries

Hee Seung Nam, MD, Orthopedic Surgeon · Area of practice

I treat fractures and ligament and tendon injuries from daily life and sports. After I moved to a general hospital in 2026, my fracture surgery volume rose sharply. I have also written about when to remove metal after a bone has healed, bone health (osteopenia) and bone infection.

  • Ligament and tendon injuries
  • Hardware removal
  • Osteopenia
