The Surgical Record in Numbers

Hee Seung Nam, MD, Orthopedic Surgeon · March 2023 to October 2026 · Updated November 1, 2026

I compiled the 1,040 operations I performed as the primary surgeon, exactly as recorded in the hospital's operating register. I want to show in numbers which areas, with which operations, and which people I have treated. It contains no personal information such as patients' names or registration numbers.

I spent three years in a spine and joint specialty hospital and one year in a general hospital. In 2026, after I moved to a general hospital that sees many trauma patients, fracture surgery rose sharply. Fracture operations, which were 67 in all of 2025, were 146 in the 10 months of 2026.

Where I operated

The knee and leg were most common at 374. Joint replacement, arthroscopy and cartilage treatment are all included here.

Next were the foot and ankle (206) and the hand and wrist (204). These are sites where fractures and ligament injuries from bracing a fall or twisting are common.

The shoulder and upper arm account for 145, mostly arthroscopic rotator cuff repair and fractures of the humerus and clavicle.

Ages 60–79 were most common, and those 80 and over were 74 (about 10%). There were also 12 people over 90 and 21 children and adolescents under 18. Men (385) and women (378) were about equal. Age and sex are based on the 763 operations for which this information was recorded.

What lies behind the numbers

The number of operations does not directly speak to skill. Cases where I advised not operating, even for the same disease, are not in these numbers, and such judgments are often harder than operating.

But each of these 1,040 operations has a photo folder, and after the folder's name there is a short note such as “next time, like this.” I believe those notes, more than the numbers, make the next patient's operation a little better. A surgeon's “I should have...” notes →

About this record

  • Source: operations recorded with Hee Seung Nam as the primary surgeon in each hospital's operating room register. Busan Himchan Hospital (March 2023 to December 2024), Bupyeong Himchan Hospital (2025), Incheon Himchan General Hospital (January to October 2026).
  • Classification: each operation was classified as one main operation by the name of the operation. Even when several procedures were done at once, it was counted as one, and some numbers can differ a little depending on the classification criteria.
  • Age and sex: the 2025 records do not have this item, so the figures are based on 763 operations from 2023, 2024 and 2026.
  • Personal information: nothing that could identify a person, such as names, registration numbers and operation dates, is included.
  • This page is meant to describe my clinical experience; it does not guarantee treatment results and is not for comparison with other physicians or medical institutions.
