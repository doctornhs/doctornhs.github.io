Foot & Ankle

Hee Seung Nam, MD, Orthopedic Surgeon · Area of practice

I treat hallux valgus (bunion), ankle fractures and pain, fifth metatarsal fractures and diabetic foot. The foot and ankle are common sites of fractures and ligament injuries from falls and sprains.
