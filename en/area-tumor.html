Lumps & Tumors

Hee Seung Nam, MD, Orthopedic Surgeon · Area of practice

Most lumps you can feel are benign, but very rarely a malignant tumor called a sarcoma is mixed in. During residency at the Korea Cancer Center Hospital I cared for patients with bone and soft tissue tumors, and I have published in this field (Research). I remove lumps such as lipomas, ganglion cysts and epidermoid cysts and send them for biopsy.

Conditions I treat

  • Lipoma, ganglion cyst, epidermoid cyst
  • Lumps that need a biopsy
  • Telling a soft tissue sarcoma apart
